   - Caribbean Maritime: Small-world route network, density=0.0358 (3,737x denser than rail).
     Giant SCC has diameter=6 hops, avg path length=2.498 hops. Top hub: Port Everglades (degree 280).

C. Forecasting Targets & Loss Function Trade-off:
   - Target (7d future sum): Mean=16.95, Std=44.66, Median=5.0, P99=185.0, Max=1,188, Zeros=18.74%.
   - Skewness=9.53, Kurtosis=137.68. MSE/L2 risks catastrophic gradient explosion on megahubs.
   - Recommended: Smooth L1 Loss (Huber beta=1.0) with log1p(y) target transformation.

D. Dynamic Predictive Signal vs Multicollinearity:
   - Pooled correlation with target (r=0.959) is confounded by between-port scale.
   - Within-port correlation isolates true day-to-day signal, peaking at rolling 7d mean (r=0.448).
   - Severe multicollinearity between 7d, 14d, and 30d rolling means (r > 0.995).
     Downstream models must retain 7d mean and encode wider windows as residuals (roll7 - roll30).

E. Operational Dwell Firewall:
   - Strict separation maintained between vessel anchorage waiting, berth service, and container yard dwell.
   - ADB Data: Neopanamax waiting surged +171% in 2021 (6.6h to 17.9h; >100h at LA/LB).
   - Zenodo AIS: Monthly vessel dwell correlates with ADB turnaround at r=+0.8373 (LA/LB) and -0.1294 (NY/NJ) (Table 22).
   - Mendeley Data: Red channel physical customs inspection doubles container yard dwell (3.18d to 5.85d).

F. Chronological Target-Window Split Stability (L=30, H=7):
   - Train: 3,696,350 sequences (71.34%, 2019-01-30 to 2023-12-24, target ends 2023-12-31)
