# Strategy for 0.90 Macro-F1 (Three-Class Dwell Prediction)

**Status:** proposal for sign-off. Nothing in this document has been evaluated on the holdout.
**Evidence base:** `predictability_and_ceiling_analysis.md` (all numbers are mean-of-folds Macro-F1 on the selection-aware cohort, three embargoed rolling-origin dev folds, scored from saved predictions).

---

## 0. The answer in one paragraph

0.90 Macro-F1 **with all three classes still open at prediction time** is not reachable with the current data at any prediction time we can construct. Measured: 0.40 at discharge, 0.48 at +12 h, 0.52 at +24 h, 0.54 at +48 h. Even an oracle that knows, at discharge, the exact customs-release date *and* delivery-job time reaches only **0.83**. The residual is pickup behaviour after the delivery job (median 13 h, p90 52 h), which nothing in the extract predicts. A credible path to 0.90 therefore needs **(i) new upstream data that forecasts customs release and delivery-order release, and (ii) pickup/appointment information**, used at a prediction time when that information exists. The single most informative next step is the extended terminal extract plus customs-declaration (PIB) timestamps. Track A (current data) is worth finishing for a defensible 0.45–0.55 product but cannot reach 0.90.

Read as: forecasting customs release perfectly is worth about +0.31 over the discharge model. Adding perfect delivery-order timing is worth +0.12 more. The last ~0.07 to 0.90 needs pickup timing. These are empirical bounds for this model family and protocol, not mathematical limits.

## Track C — Improve the prediction formulation

| Formulation | Measured | Lead time (median) | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: at discharge | 0.398 | full stay | Most useful operationally, least information |
| B: fixed landmarks 12/24/48 h | 0.479 / 0.520 / 0.542 | ~40 h | Clean single-row-per-stay design; all classes open up to 65 h; **recommended primary formulation** |
| C: at customs release known | 0.683 overall, **0.455 on stays where all three classes are still possible** | 23 h | Headline number is inflated by stays for which elapsed time already rules out ON_TIME; not acceptable as a 0.90 claim |
| E: at delivery-job creation | 0.826 overall, **0.477 open subset** (CRITICAL F1 0.0 there) | 12.6 h | Looks close to target but is mostly bookkeeping; the clearest example of a misleading 0.8+ |
| Dynamic multi-landmark (score every 12 h until exit) | not run | — | Operationally best; evaluate per landmark on the open subset, never pooled across landmarks |

**Rule for every formulation:** the 0.90 criterion is evaluated only on stays for which all three classes are still possible at prediction time (elapsed < 65.11 h), with the eligible population, prediction timestamp and censoring treatment fixed in advance. Reporting Macro-F1 over stays whose class is already determined by elapsed time is not a forecasting result.

**Alternative target (separate experiment only).** Thresholds are p50/p85 of a biased window. An operationally anchored target (e.g. ≤ 72 h / 72–168 h / > 168 h, or thresholds refit on the clean cohort) may be more meaningful, but must be agreed with operations first, be reported side by side with the original target, and never replace it in the headline.

## Track D — Modelling approaches worth the effort

1. **Cumulative / landmark-hazard models.** Model P(exit by 65.1 h | in yard at t) and P(exit by 160.9 h | in yard at t) as two monotone binary models (Frank–Hall or discrete-time hazard on 12 h bins with landmarking). This respects the ordinal target, shares one model across landmarks, and handles "still in yard" naturally.
2. **Two-stage process model** (best fit to the causal chain): (a) forecast hours-to-customs-release, (b) hours customs → delivery job, (c) hours job → gate-out, each with quantile gradient boosting; combine by Monte-Carlo convolution into class probabilities. Each stage can be fed by its own new data source (B2, B3, B4), so data value is measurable stage by stage.
3. **Prior-shift–aware decision rule** (A3) instead of fixed multipliers.
4. **Regime/mixture-of-experts:** evaluate only after B1 provides more than one congestion episode; with one episode the gating cannot be validated.
5. **Not recommended:** further XGBoost/LightGBM hyperparameter sweeps, resampling (ROS/RUS/SMOTE), deeper trees, stacking on the current features. The registry shows these move Macro-F1 by ±0.02, inside the bootstrap interval width (±0.04).

## Track E — Evaluation system (pre-registered)

1. **Population:** selection-aware cohort (discharges ≥ extract start, ≥ 600 h follow-up before extract end). With B1, also include stays unfinished at extraction as right-censored rows. They are labelled CRITICAL if still in the yard 160.9 h after discharge, otherwise excluded only from the window where the class is undetermined.
2. **Development:** rolling-origin folds on discharge time with a 7-day label embargo; all feature, model, and decision-rule choices are made here only. Track the number of configurations compared.
3. **Holdout TEST (current data):** discharges 2021-08-27 → 2021-09-05, model trained on discharges < 2021-08-20. Scored **once** per pre-declared final candidate per formulation.
4. **Independent confirmation:** months from the extended extract (B1) that no one has seen. A 0.90 claim is made only on this set.
5. **Success criterion:** Macro-F1 ≥ 0.90 on the open-class population at the declared prediction time, with the 95% vessel-call cluster-bootstrap lower bound reported; per-class F1, CRITICAL recall, balanced accuracy, weighted F1, accuracy, CRITICAL PR-AUC and confusion matrix all from the same saved array; no stay excluded after the fact.
6. **Integrity checks in CI:** future-event perturbation test for every feature (exists: `tests/test_reassessment.py`), embargo/holdout isolation tests, metric-consistency assertion inside the scorer.

**What would falsify the 0.90 plan:** if, with PIB lane/submission data, the forecast of hours-to-customs-release explains less than ~70% of its variance, the discharge-time model cannot exceed the 0.71 oracle and 0.90 is out of reach before release. If truck-appointment data are unobtainable, 0.90 is out of reach at any landmark with all classes open, and the best defensible target becomes ≈ 0.80 at a post-release landmark.
